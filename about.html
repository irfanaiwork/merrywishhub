🎄 About Merry Wish Hub

Merry Wish Hub is a free Christmas fun site. Make a greeting card with your name, take the Christmas character quiz, and share festive wishes with friends on WhatsApp and Facebook.

Everything here is free and needs no sign-up. Cards are made in your own browser — nothing you type is sent to us or stored. Quiz results are just for fun.

Some pages may show sponsored content from our advertising partners. That is how this free site stays free.
